Programme Description

The Bachelor of Computer Science programme provides students with a strong foundation in software development, programming, cybersecurity, networking, artificial intelligence, databases, and cloud computing.


Students will complete practical projects, industrial training, and collaborative assignments to prepare for careers in the technology industry.
