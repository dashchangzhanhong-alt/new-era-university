Certificate in Practical Computer & IT Support

Build essential technical skills required to diagnose hardware, troubleshoot network connectivity, and deliver hands-on IT support.

Programme Overview

Duration

1 Year (Full-Time / Part-Time Options)

Campus

New Era Institute of Vocational & Continuing Education, Kuala Lumpur

Qualification

Vocational Certificate

Mode

Practical Workshop & Hands-on Lab

Programme Description

The Certificate in Practical Computer & IT Support equips students with core competencies in computer assembly, OS configuration, peripherals maintenance, basic local area network setup, and customer-facing technical support workflows.


Students spend a significant portion of class hours inside technical labs tackling simulated real-world hardware failures and software troubleshooting scenarios.

Core Modules

Hardware Assembly & Repair

PC components, motherboard diagnostics, power units, and upgrades

Operating Systems Support

Windows/Linux installation, configuration, user account management, and backups

Networking Fundamentals

Router configurations, Wi-Fi setups, cabling, and basic troubleshooting

Helpdesk Operations

Ticketing workflows, customer service ethics, and remote diagnostic tools

Entry Requirements

  • Completed secondary education (SPM / O-Level or equivalent)
  • Pass an interview or basic aptitude screening
  • Keen interest in technology and hands-on maintenance
