Admission Information

Find out everything you need to know about joining our programmes, entry requirements, and application procedures.

Admission Overview

Intake Periods

January, May, and September intakes available.

Campus Location

New Era Institute of Vocational & Continuing Education, Kuala Lumpur

Entry Pathways

Open to SPM/O-Level holders and working adults via APEL.

Application Mode

Online Application or In-Person Registration

Admission Procedure

Applying to New Era Institute is simple. Prospective students can submit their applications online or visit our campus for counseling and registration assistance. Our admissions team is ready to guide you through course selection and fee structures.


Ensure all academic transcripts and identification documents are prepared prior to submitting your enrollment form.

Application Steps

1. Choose Programme

Explore our vocational courses and select your preferred field of study

2. Check Requirements

Ensure you meet the basic academic or work-experience criteria

3. Submit Application

Fill out the online application form or visit our admissions office

4. Enrollment & Fees

Receive your offer letter and complete fee arrangements to secure your spot

General Entry Requirements

  • Completed secondary education (SPM / O-Level or equivalent)
  • Pass an interview or basic aptitude screening where required
  • Alternative entry routes available via work experience (APEL)
